# PROOF Room demo script for judges

About 5 minutes. Live at **https://dylanmorenow.github.io/PROOF/**

## Before you present

- Open the link in Chrome or Edge on a 1920 × 1080 screen, full screen with F11.
- Click **About** in the sidebar, then **Reset demo data**, so every number starts clean. Then click **Sign out** in the same dialog so you open on the sign-in screen.
- If the "New here?" bubble appears, dismiss it. You will start the tour yourself.
- Keys worth knowing. **L** toggles the Strategy lens. **[** collapses the sidebar. **Esc** exits a tour. **← →** move through a tour.
- Every company, person and figure is fictional. Say so once at the start.

## 0. Sign in (20 s)

**Show** the sign-in screen. Let the globe settle while the demo account types itself in.

**Say** "Every PT Energy customer gets their own PROOF Room. Here is Mahakam Delta Processing signing in."

**Do** click **Sign in**. The camera dives into Indonesia and the workspace opens. No password is checked, it is a prototype.

## 1. The command center (60 s)

**Show** the Overview.

**Say** "PROOF Room is the customer workspace behind our P.R.O.O.F. strategy. A PT Energy customer opens it and sees, in one sentence, 24 active operations across 4 regions, 7 open exceptions, and 3 jobs waiting for their sign-off."

**Point at** the five KPI tiles. On-time delivery, service levels, and evidence verified within 48 hours. "Every number is computed from the same records you will see in a moment, nothing is typed in."

**Do** switch the account in the top right to *Mahakam Delta Processing*. Every number changes. Switch back to *All accounts*.

## 2. Option B on the map (40 s)

**Do** hover **Balikpapan** on the map, then click it.

**Say** "Option B, Archipelago Delivery Resilience. Four proposed regional hubs, Batam, Surabaya, Balikpapan and Sorong. Each shows its workload, on-time rate, open exceptions and how many local vendors are qualified."

**Do** press **L**. Each module gets a tag showing which strategy option it delivers. Press **L** again to hide them.

## 3. PIP and the guided tour (40 s)

**Do** click **PIP**, the crystal in the bottom-right corner. The tour for the page you are on starts right away. Go through two or three steps with **→**. Every page has its own tour.

**Say** "PIP is our onboarding guide. A new customer learns the workspace in about a minute, and every step is one plain sentence on what this is and why it matters."

**Do** press **Esc**. The page returns exactly as it was.

## 4. From a number to its proof (90 s)

**Do** in *Needs attention*, open **Acknowledge completed service · WO-26-0417**.

**Say** "This is Option C, Digital-First Customer Experience. One gas turbine inspection in Balikpapan. The crew, the qualified vendor, every milestone with real dates. One step slipped a day and it shows in amber, we do not hide delays."

**Point at** the Evidence list. "Five records. Each carries its Proof Chain, captured, submitted, verified, acknowledged. Uploaded is not the same as verified."

**Do** click **Hot-section inspection report**. Show the version history. "Version 1 was rejected because a signature was missing. Version 2 was verified."

**Do** go back, then click **Acknowledge service**. PIP reacts, the job closes, and the Overview numbers update.

## 5. Performance and reporting (30 s)

**Do** open **Performance**. Show the period comparison and the trend. Click **Export report**.

**Say** "The same data becomes a monthly report the customer can print or save as PDF, no slide deck needed."

## 6. The independent idea, Paid-on-Proof (60 s)

**Do** open **Outcome Ledger** and click **Run calculation**.

**Say** "This is a separate, independent proposal. It is not part of the Option C investment and nothing here is live. Part of the fee is paid only for verified results. The baseline is 90% availability, verified availability is 96%, so 6 points at 1,000 dollars each adds 6,000 to a 20,000 fixed fee. The draft is 26,000 and still needs customer and commercial approval."

**Point at** the Evidence card. "The 96% comes from three monthly logs, and each one opens in the Evidence Center. Every dollar traces back to proof."

**Do** select **Cooling-Water System Availability**. "If performance falls below baseline, only the fixed fee is paid."

## Close (10 s)

**Say** "From price-led bidding to proof-led growth. Every operation, every milestone, every proof."

## Likely questions

| Question | Short answer |
|---|---|
| Is this connected to real systems? | No. It is a frontend prototype with fictional data, built to show the experience. |
| Are the four hubs real PT Energy sites? | No. They are proposed candidate nodes under Option B. |
| What does "verified" mean? | Checked against its source. The site, the timestamp, the signatory and the file itself. |
| Is Paid-on-Proof part of the digital investment? | No. It is an independent concept and would need its own business case and contracts. |
| Does it work on a phone? | Yes. Open the link on a phone, and the tour switches to a bottom sheet. |

## If something goes wrong

- A tour looks stuck. Press **Esc** and start it again from PIP.
- Numbers look different from this script. Use **About → Reset demo data**.
- No internet. Use the screenshots in `docs/screenshots` as a backup deck.
